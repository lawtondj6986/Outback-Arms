How to Buy Your First Firearm in Massachusetts

Massachusetts has some of the strictest gun laws in the country — but the path to your first firearm is simpler than the internet makes it sound. Here's the plain-English version, from a family shop that walks people through it every week.

⚠️ Quick note: Massachusetts modernized its firearm laws in 2024, and the rules keep evolving. This guide is a plain-English starting point, not legal advice. Your local police department is the authority on your application, and our staff verifies the current requirements with you before any sale. When in doubt, call us at 781-585-6615.

Step 1 — Take a state-approved safety course

Before you can apply for any Massachusetts firearm license, you need a certificate from a state-approved firearms safety course. This is the single step that trips most first-timers up — you can't apply without it.

Good news: Outback Arms runs Mass-approved safety classes every Saturday. You'll leave with the exact certificate you need to apply, plus real range time and straight answers to your questions — no judgment, no rushing.

Step 2 — Figure out which license you need (LTC vs FID)

Massachusetts issues two main firearm licenses:

  • LTC (License to Carry) — the broader license most buyers pursue.
  • FID (Firearm Identification card) — a more limited license.

They cover different firearms and carry different requirements, and the specifics shifted with the 2024 reform. Rather than hand you rules that might be out of date, we'll sit down with you for five minutes and figure out exactly which one fits what you want to do — target shooting, hunting, home defense, carry. It's one of our favorite conversations.

Step 3 — Apply at your local police department

With your safety certificate in hand, you apply through your city or town's police department — they're the licensing authority. You'll complete an application, provide ID, and typically do fingerprinting and an interview. Processing times vary by department, so apply early.

Step 4 — Come shop (the fun part)

Once you're licensed, you're ready to buy. Every firearm in Massachusetts is purchased in person, with a valid license and an in-store background check. At Outback Arms you can:

  • Browse our in-stock guns online and hold one — reserve it free, pick it up when you're ready.
  • Have a firearm shipped to us for transfer if you found it elsewhere.
  • Ask anything — we only stock MA-compliant firearms, so you won't fall in love with something you can't take home.

A few common questions

Do I need a license to buy ammo? In Massachusetts, yes — a valid firearm license is required to purchase ammunition. Bring your license.

How long does licensing take? It varies by police department — anywhere from a few weeks to a few months. Starting your safety class now is the best way to get the clock moving.

Can I buy online and ship to my house? No — firearms can't be shipped to a Massachusetts home. They go to a licensed dealer (like us) for in-person pickup with your license and a background check.
